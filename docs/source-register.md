## Required fields before a source is approved

- Provider / responsible organization
- Exact dataset or product name
- Stable product URL and download/service endpoint
- Access date
- Publication date, version, or observation period
- Geographic coverage
- Scale or spatial resolution
- Coordinate reference system and vertical datum where relevant
- License and terms URL, with a plain-language interpretation
- Attribution text
- Whether redistribution and derivative works are permitted
- Acquisition method and checksum
- Known limitations
- Decision: APPROVED / HOLD / REJECTED, with reviewer and date

## Initial rights rule

If the license or terms cannot be established, status is **HOLD**. Do not place the data in a distributable build merely because it can be viewed or downloaded.
