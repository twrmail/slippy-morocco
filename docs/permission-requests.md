# Rabat data permission requests

These are draft messages for the two candidate data providers. The recipient addresses below were checked against the providers' published contact information on 15 November 2026.

## 1. CNER — classified road network

**To:** cner@mtpnet.gov.ma  
**Organization:** Centre National d'Études et de Recherches Routières (CNER)  
**Contact source:** https://www.cner.ma/contact.html

**Subject:** Request for Rabat road-network GIS data and reuse conditions

Bonjour,

I am working on a small independent mapping project focused initially on the roads and waterways of Rabat.

I found the CNER PR Tracker and its reference to the official January 2025 *Tableau des Routes*. I would like to ask whether CNER can make the classified road network for the Rabat area available as GIS vector data.

If so, could you please clarify:

1. Whether a GIS dataset or export is available for Rabat, including national, regional and provincial roads where possible.
2. The source date, coordinate reference system, attribute definitions and geographic coverage.
3. Whether the data may be used for research and comparison with OpenStreetMap.
4. Whether permission can be granted to adapt the data and display it in an independently operated public web map.
5. Whether redistribution of the original or modified data is permitted, and under what attribution or licensing conditions.

I understand that access to the PR Tracker does not necessarily imply permission to reuse or redistribute its underlying data. I would appreciate written clarification of the applicable terms before proceeding.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Cordialement,

[Your name]

## 2. SIG Maroc — roads and hydrography

**To:** support@sig-maroc.com  
**Organization:** SIG Maroc  
**Contact source:** https://www.sig-maroc.com/legales/conditions-utilisation

**Subject:** Question about provenance and reuse rights for road and hydrography datasets

Bonjour,

I am exploring the creation of a small independent map of Rabat, Morocco, initially limited to roads and water features.

I found the road-network and hydrography vector layers in the SIG Maroc data catalog and interactive map. The ability to extract a geographic area is very useful for this project.

Before downloading or using these datasets, I would appreciate clarification on their provenance and permitted uses.

For the **Réseau Routier Complet**, **Réseau Hydrographique (Cours d'eau)** and **Zones de plans d'eau** layers:

1. Who originally produced each dataset, and what are the underlying sources?
2. What are the dates, scales or levels of detail, coordinate reference systems and attribute definitions?
3. Are the datasets intended to be freely reusable, or does “free download” refer only to access?
4. Do the applicable terms permit modification, comparison with OpenStreetMap, public display in an independently operated web map and redistribution of derived data?
5. I also found a separate Roads (SIG Maroc) listing on Kharita that declares a CC BY 4.0 license. Does that license apply to the road data you provide, and is there an equivalent license or permission for the hydrography layers?

I noticed the SIG Maroc terms of use refer to prior authorization for reproduction, distribution or publication. I would therefore like to obtain explicit clarification before using or publishing any of the data.

Thank you for helping clarify this.

Cordialement,

[Your name]

---

## Rights handling

Until written clarification is received:

- CNER classified-road data: do not download or redistribute.
- SIG Maroc roads and water layers: do not download or redistribute.
- January 2025 community road shapefile: preserve as a comparison candidate; provenance and reuse rights remain unresolved.
- OpenStreetMap: use as the working base under ODbL 1.0, with attribution and applicable share-alike obligations.

These messages request both data access and explicit permission. A reply granting access alone should not be treated as permission to republish.
