# Slippy Morocco

A separate, source-traceable geographic foundation for Rabat and its surrounding area.

## First milestone: understand the ground

The initial work is a base map, not a visitor product. It will establish a reliable picture of:

- terrain and elevation;
- coastline, rivers, estuaries, and other water;
- broad land cover;
- the built environment and transport network;
- place names and administrative geography.

Tourism, tours, language lessons, stadium guides, and visitor services are future layers. They must not become prerequisites for a sound geographic base.

## Data principles

1. Every dataset must have a recorded source, acquisition date, version or checksum where available, and license/terms assessment.
2. Publicly viewable does not automatically mean downloadable or reusable. No source is redistributed until its terms are understood.
3. Keep raw source data separate from derived data. Derived outputs must be reproducible from documented inputs and steps.
4. Keep observations from local contributors distinct from authoritative source data. Record evidence and review before incorporating corrections.
5. Do not treat unknown, unverified, or unavailable data as confirmed.
6. Keep this repository independent from Pennsylvania Slippy projects and their data.

## Working geography

The initial study area is Rabat–Salé and the Bouregreg corridor, with enough surrounding context to understand the Atlantic coast and connected urban geography. The exact boundary remains provisional until it is grounded in documented geographic boundaries and tested against source coverage.

## Status

Repository initialized. No geographic source data has yet been acquired or validated. The first implementation milestone will document the study area, source candidates, licensing questions, and validation requirements before building a rendered map.
